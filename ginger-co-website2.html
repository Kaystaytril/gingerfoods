Our Story

A little place
with a big heart

Ginger & Co is East London's beloved neighbourhood café, deli, and bakery all rolled into one warm, welcoming space on Frere Road. We've been feeding the city with handcrafted food, freshly baked goods, and exceptional coffee since the day we opened our doors.

Everything we make is prepared with care — from our signature breakfast bagels and hand-rolled pasta lasagna, to our showstopping Turkish Delight cheesecake that has become something of a local legend. Come in to grab a quick takeaway or settle in and soak up the atmosphere.

We believe great food should be fresh, honest, and made with love. That's our promise — every single day.

What We Offer

More than
just a café

We're a full deli experience — a café, a bakery, and a gift shop all in one beautifully curated space. Whether you're in for five minutes or an hour, there's always something to delight you.

01
Café & Coffee

World-class coffee roasted by the East London Coffee Company. Our house blend, Ginger Joe, is the one to order. Single origin options always available.

02
Deli & Meals

Fresh, honest food made daily. Generous portions, beautiful presentation, and flavours that keep you coming back — from breakfast through to lunch.

03
Bakery

Freshly baked goods every single day. From our famous chocolate and Turkish Delight cheesecakes to artisan bread, pastries, and biscuits.

04
Gift Shop

A curated little shop inside the café — beautiful homewares, rustic décor, and artisan pantry products that make the perfect gift.
